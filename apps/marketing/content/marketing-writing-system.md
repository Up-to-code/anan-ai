# Marketing Writing System

## Purpose
- Keep all public Anan copy aligned with the company story, SEO requirements, and the business architecture of the platform.

## Landing Page Formula
- Start with the category: what Anan is.
- State the business problem in market terms.
- Explain what changes across assistant, workspace, and developer surfaces.
- Show why this matters commercially.
- End with a clear action: assistant or workspace.

## Product Page Formula
- Problem
- What Anan changes
- Why it matters commercially
- Who it is for
- CTA

## Tone Rules
- Clear over clever
- Commercial over abstract
- Specific over inflated
- Infrastructure over feature dumping
- Trustworthy over theatrical

## SEO Rules
- Each indexable page needs a distinct title and description.
- Every localized page should expose canonical and language alternates.
- Keep locale URLs stable under `/ar`, `/en`, and `/fr`.
- Use organization/site structured data where it helps search engines understand the brand.
- Do not rely on deprecated geo metadata. Use visible regional language and market context instead.

## Voice Rules
- Do not say "AI" without saying what the AI does for the business.
- Do not say "workspace" without saying what operational friction it removes.
- Do not say "integration" without saying what capability it unlocks.
- Prefer verbs like qualify, route, coordinate, distribute, measure, and scale.

## Content Blocks We Reuse Often
- "The assistant turns conversation into qualified demand."
- "The workspace gives every team the same commercial picture."
- "Developer tooling keeps project data, offers, and distribution connected."
- "Anan is the operating layer between demand and execution."

## Trust Page Rules
- About page explains category, mission, values, and operating thesis.
- Team page should communicate capability and responsibility, not vanity biography.
- Contact page should clarify the right entry path into the business.
- Legal pages should stay direct and readable.

## Blog and Docs Rules
- Blog intros should connect technical or product notes back to commercial outcomes.
- Docs metadata should still reinforce that integrations are part of a wider operating system.
- Technical depth is welcome, but framing should never sound disconnected from the product story.

## Review Checklist
- Does the page sound like a company, not an internal dashboard?
- Does it explain what changes for the business?
- Does it make assistant, workspace, and developer roles distinct?
- Does the CTA match the page intent?
- Does the metadata say something unique and useful?
