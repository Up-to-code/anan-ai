# Anan General Platform Brief

## Purpose

This document explains Anan in normal language for anyone who is not approaching the company as an investor.

Use it for:

- events
- product introductions
- partnership conversations
- customer-facing explanations
- general deck writing
- “What is Anan?” and “Why Anan?” sections

This is not an investor memo. It should help anyone understand the product, the vision, and why it matters.

## Overview

Anan is an AI-first real estate operating system.

It brings together two connected parts of the experience:

- a buyer-side assistant that helps people search, understand, finance, and move forward in their real-estate journey
- an agent-first workspace where developers and brokers operate the real work from the first screen

Instead of using one tool for discovery, another for finance, another for messaging, another for CRM, and another for follow-up, Anan connects those steps into one system.

## What Anan Is

Anan is not just a chatbot, not just a marketplace, and not just a CRM.

It is a real-estate product built to connect:

- buyer demand
- financing and qualification
- broker collaboration
- developer execution
- market visibility

The product starts with AI, but it does not end with AI. The assistant is the start of the workflow, and the workspace is where the real work continues.

## The Two Main Product Surfaces

### 1. The buyer-side assistant

The buyer journey starts from an assistant-first experience.

In the current product, that includes:

- property-aware conversation
- property search
- property detail review
- finance-related guidance
- bank offers and financing comparison
- mortgage and installment estimation
- advisor handoff

This side of Anan is designed to help people move forward, not just browse passively.

The important point is that the buyer journey is not only about finding a property. It also helps the user understand affordability, compare financing options, and move into the next real step with an advisor or broker.

### 2. The workspace

The workspace is the operating surface for developers and brokers.

Its main entry point is not just a dashboard. The workspace starts with an assistant-first screen where the user can ask for work directly.

From that first screen, the workspace can already help with operational tasks such as:

- projects
- offers
- CRM and client context
- broker-related analysis
- market analysis
- follow-up actions

The workspace also uses structured assistant surfaces for drafts, approvals, missing data, insights, and execution states. That means the assistant is not only answering questions. It is helping prepare and move work.

It is where teams manage:

- overview and settings
- projects
- offers
- CRM
- inbox collaboration
- market activity

This is the part of the system where conversations become action.

## Vision

The vision behind Anan is simple:

real estate should not feel like disconnected chats, spreadsheets, listings, and follow-up tools.

Anan is being built around one connected operating logic where:

- AI understands demand
- AI helps qualify and guide the financial path
- the workspace holds execution
- brokers and teams stay in the same flow
- context is not lost between one step and the next

When we say “one screen” or “one system,” the meaning is not that everything must live on one literal page. The meaning is that the product should feel connected, consistent, and continuous from the first conversation to the work that follows.

## Why Anan

Real estate still suffers from broken flow:

- interest starts in one place
- financing happens somewhere else
- the conversation moves somewhere else
- the follow-up happens in another tool
- the team loses context between steps

Anan exists to connect that flow.

The assistant is not only for answering questions.
The workspace is not only for storing records.
Together, they create one product where demand, financing, collaboration, and execution stay connected.

## Who Anan Is For

Anan is for three sides of the real-estate experience:

- buyers who want a smarter and more guided journey
- brokers who want better coordination and less lost work
- developers who want one place to manage projects, offers, teams, and visibility

The value comes from connecting those sides instead of treating them as separate products.

## How It Works

At a simple level, the flow is:

1. A buyer starts with the assistant.
2. The assistant helps the buyer search, review property context, understand financing, and move closer to a decision.
3. That journey connects into the workspace where brokers and developers can execute the next steps.
4. The workspace agent helps teams work through projects, offers, CRM, collaboration, and market analysis from one connected operating flow.
5. Handoff, follow-up, and execution stay inside the same product system instead of being scattered across tools.

That is the heart of Anan: turning interest into structured real-estate execution.

## What Makes It Different

Most real-estate products solve only one part of the problem.

They may be:

- search tools
- finance tools
- CRMs
- chat tools
- broker tools
- marketplace products

Anan is different because it tries to connect the full operating flow:

- AI entry
- search and understanding
- financing and qualification
- agent-first execution workspace
- broker support
- CRM and inbox flow
- market visibility

That is why Anan should be explained as an operating system, not just a feature or a point solution.
